FreshApples

FreshApples is a moderated movie-review community where people can discover movies, share reviews, and read clearly labelled AI-generated summaries. Administrators maintain the catalogue and handle reports through a separate interface.

Deployment status: The product website is available on GitHub Pages. The Next.js application is a separate deployment and its Vercel link will be added here after release.
